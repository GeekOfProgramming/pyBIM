Gmail Installation Instructions

  1. Click Copy Signature above next to the desired account (or select and copy the rendered signature box on the light canvas).
  2. Open Gmail and click the Gear icon (top right) → See all settings.
  3. Under the General tab, scroll down to the Signature section.
  4. Click Create new, enter a name (e.g. pyBIM Official), and click in the signature editor.
  5. Press Ctrl+V (Windows) or Cmd+V (Mac) to paste the rendered signature.
  6. Under Signature defaults, select your newly created signature for For new emails use and On reply/forward use.
  7. Scroll to the bottom of the page and click Save Changes.
  8. Compose a new message to verify the visual presentation.
